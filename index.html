01 — The Band

WHO IS
ATRV2?

ATRV2 didn't start as a band project. It started with family.

The story goes back to the late 1990s, when our dad — a musician himself in his younger years — started pushing his sons to play music together in front of family and friends.

Years later, while planning a surprise party for our mom, old friends came back into the picture. Lalo returned on vocals, Juanito joined on bass, Raúl picked up the guitar, and what was supposed to be one night of 50s and 60s songs turned into something we wanted to keep doing.

ATRV2 was born — a new version of Los Atrevidos, carrying the history forward while bringing together rock, norteño, cumbia, 80s music and everything each member brought with them.
